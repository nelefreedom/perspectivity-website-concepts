What we do

How can we help you?

We bring people with different knowledge, interests and perspectives together. This is how we create space for new insights and responses to the complex challenges we are facing.

Facilitation of multi-stakeholder processes

We facilitate dialogue with diverse stakeholders around shared challenges. Strengthening common ground and including diverse perspectives brings depth, new insights and room for collective action.

Narrative research and evaluation

To unravel patterns in complexity, we use the power of stories for research and evaluation, in a participatory and inclusive way. We help you learn from the past and decide for the future.

Leadership and capacity strengthening

To help you and your organisation navigate complexity, we offer group training and coaching on the job, with tools and methods for complex situations.

The building blocks include backbone, responsive leadership, reinforcing actions, involvement of all, urgency for change, vital connections, adaptive learning and shared ambitions.

Our vision and tool

The Complexity Navigator

nine building blocks for collective impact in complexity

To navigate complexity and create collective impact, you need to pay attention to nine crucial aspects. These building blocks help you unravel your specific situation and find your starting point for action. The Navigator is based on proven methods and our years of collective experience.

Our tools

Made by Perspectivity

Two tools that grew out of our work, now used far beyond it.

Stories and stats, connected in one tool

Sprockler is our online tool for narrative research. People share a story and interpret it themselves; the patterns appear right away in interactive graphs, with the individual stories at the heart of every graph. Used in evaluations from Timor-Leste to Amsterdam.

Visit sprockler.com (external site)

The Perspectivity Challenge

A serious board game about complexity

In The Perspectivity Challenge, players act as world leaders and experience complexity first-hand, then reflect on what they learned. Games on climate, food, public health, fashion, human security and decision making. Played in 24 countries, by 83 organisations.

Visit the Challenge (external site)
